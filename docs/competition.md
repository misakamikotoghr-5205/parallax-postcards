# Through Your Eyes: First-Person Game Jam

Checked 2026-11-05 against the [official itch.io jam page](https://itch.io/jam/through-your-eyes-first-person-game-jam), hosted by UnclePhilburt.

The event accepts people of any experience, solo developers or teams, any engine, browser or downloadable games, and playable prototypes. Existing and new games are both allowed. The central constraint is a primarily first-person experience. Content must be legally distributable, with third-party credits when required; itch.io community rules also apply.

The page states submissions close **November 15, 2026**. Its extracted schedule displays `2026-11-15 17:24:24`, and the raw date value has no explicit timezone or UTC offset. We do **not** convert this into a claimed Beijing/UTC deadline. The owner should use the official page's account/browser countdown and submit early. The body lists community voting through November 22. The page describes recognition and community ratings, not a guaranteed cash prize.

Parallax Postcards is a new, first-person playable observation puzzle. The primary interaction stays in the player's eye view; the small survey map is an optional navigation aid. Three rooms have objectives, geometric alignment, collection progression, hints, undo/reset and a complete ending. The software renderer supports browsers without WebGL.
