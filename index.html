PARALLAX POSTCARDSWALK SLOWLY. LOOK AGAIN.

A small field guide

Three rooms contain pictures broken across different distances. They only come together from one point of view.

  1. Walk toward the floating envelope and face it. Press E or Read the letter.
  2. Find the brass cross on the floor. The letter tells you which way to face.
  3. Move and turn until the picture aligns. Press Space or Capture postcard.
  4. Each collected postcard opens the next room. All three finish the journey.

Keyboard: W/S forward/back; A/D strafe; arrow keys turn; Shift + turn adjusts slowly. Drag the view to turn. Screen buttons work with touch, mouse and keyboard; hold them to keep moving.

Use Undo, Reset position, three levels of hints, or the optional survey map whenever you like. No pointer-lock permission, WebGL, audio, account or network service is required.
